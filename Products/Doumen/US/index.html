Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 22, 2026

1. On-Device Processing

Doumen is operated by the individual developer ZhangXu (Chinese name: 张旭; brand name: Xuyu Tech) and does not require registration or login.

Images you import, patterns you create, project names, edits, inventory and making progress, export settings, and language and appearance preferences are stored on your device by default and are not uploaded to developer-operated servers. Image cropping, color sampling, OCR-assisted import, and background cleanup are also performed on your device.

Diagnostic records remain local unless you choose to email us screenshots, diagnostic files, or other materials. We use information you send only to handle your request. Exported PNG files may contain project data and the source image; sharing the file also shares its embedded data.

2. Optional iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, project files, source assets, and sync metadata are stored in your iCloud account through Apple services. Doumen does not operate a separate project-sync server and does not receive your iCloud project content through this feature.

After sync is disabled, the current device stops syncing. Data already stored in iCloud remains under your control through Doumen, device settings, or iCloud. Apple processes this data under its terms and privacy policy.

3. System Permissions

  • System photo or file picker: choose images to process;
  • Photo-library add permission: save exported images to Photos;
  • File and sharing features: import, save, or share files; and
  • iCloud: sync projects after you enable sync.

4. Retention, Deletion, and Your Rights

You manage local projects and diagnostic records on your device. Uninstalling Doumen usually removes data in the app sandbox. Content saved to Photos, Files, iCloud, or shared with others must be managed or deleted in those locations. Support email is retained only as needed to respond, maintain necessary records, or meet legal obligations.

You can access, export, correct, or delete local data through Doumen and system features. You may also contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or other applicable privacy rights.

5. Policy Updates and Contact

If our information practices change materially, we will update this policy and seek consent again where required by law.

For questions, comments, or complaints, contact xuyutechnologies@outlook.com.
